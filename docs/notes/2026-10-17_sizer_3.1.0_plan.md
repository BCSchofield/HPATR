# Sizer 3.1.0: sizes that cannot run away, a rolling background, and a throughput that adds up

2026-10-17. Approved by Ben ("Sure, lets do this!"). It follows the CCD day re-measured at 3.0.0
(`Experiments/Taguchi/CCD/CCD sizer 3.0.0 remeasure 16-10-2026/`, README there; to-do
`2026-10-17_todo.md` items 0 and 3). Phase-gated: stop and report after each phase.

## Why

- **3.0.0 sizes a droplet from the profile around it.** Only other AI detections are blanked. Dark liquid the AI did
  not detect stays in the profile, and the size runs away to 200-430 µm. That liquid is a jet edge, a ligament, or the
  shadow of a drop on the window.
  - Every sized droplet ≥ 200 µm checked read > 1.6× its AI mask (masks 35-70 µm).
  - They are 2-3% of sized droplets but ~85% of the d³ where they occur (123534: D32 64.5 → 101.9 µm).
  - 2.2.0 rejected them by accident, with its sharpness rule.
- **A drop that lands on the window mid-run stays in every later frame.** The background is one median over the whole
  run.
- **Droplets/s (mass balance) is wrong at 3.0.0:**
  - it gives the unsized (< 30 µm) droplets' volume to the sized ones;
  - its 20-60 band straddles d_min;
  - it rests on the run's d³, which the faults dominate.
- **What an expert does.** Imaging sizers (Fdida & Blaisot; Kashdan's PDIA; LaVision ParticleMaster) put every object
  through validation filters before it is sized: isolated, detached, a uniform local background, and its size
  estimates agree. They look at the largest objects in every run.

## Decisions (Ben, 2026-10-17)

- **Go:** the three validity tests, the static-object filter, the largest-droplets sheet and the rolling background.
- **Window air purge: not an option.**
  - Tested earlier, it does not stop large high-inertia droplets.
  - It disrupts the spray: secondary atomisation, so extra droplets could not be separated from the ones the nozzle
    makes.
- **Throughput band: 30-60 µm** until the reticle brings d_min down.
- **The rate formula:** one shared function for GUI_Clean and the app.
- **The largest-droplets sheet:** one slide per run in the decks the app makes after CCD and Taguchi analysis, beside
  the extremes slides.
- **Re-analysis:**
  - Ben does it himself in the UI, for practice and to be sure it is like for like.
  - Claude gives him the steps when 3.1.0 is ready.
  - The old results must be kept.
- **To-do, needs the velocity capture:** weight each size by its speed.
- **Later, 2026-10-17 evening (after the CCD day at 3.1.0):**
  - 3.1.0 accepted; the older days are not re-measured; sizes above 155.8 µm are reported.
  - Keep on the to-do list: the app's batch running several runs at a time, for the next testing process.

## Phases

### 1. Sizing validity (measure_run; version 3.1.0)

**One liquid map per frame, independent of the AI.**
- The liquid stage's hysteresis segmentation (seed T < 0.70, grown to each piece's own half-max) moves to a shared
  module that measure_run and classical_liquid both use.
- **Blanking for the profile = every liquid pixel that is not this droplet** (not only other detections).

**Each droplet gets three tests.** Each outcome is a `size_method` of its own:
1. **`attached`:** its piece of the liquid map is much larger than its AI mask. It is a bump on a ligament, or it
   sits on a liquid body.
   - Not a free droplet: not counted.
   - In the atomised fraction its area belongs to that body (un-atomised).
2. **`dark_surround`:** with everything else blanked, the ring just outside its mask is still not clear (mean T well
   below 1).
   - Counted like a crowded droplet (added back in proportion), not sized.
3. **`inconsistent`:** its measured size disagrees with its own mask-equivalent size.
   - Normal droplets read ~0.65×; the faults read > 1.6×. The band is set from the beads and clean spray droplets.
   - Counted like crowded, not sized. A safety net behind tests 1 and 2.

**Static objects.** A "droplet" at the same position (within ~2 px) and size (±5%) in ≥ N frames is `static`: not
spray, not counted.

**`droplet_sizes.csv` gains x, y and mask_d_um,** so checks like this need no re-measuring.

**Version.** `3.1.0` through blur.DEFAULT_SIZER, process_capture, pipeline_spec, jobstate, GUI_Clean and the app's
refusals. 3.0.0 stays selectable.

**Checks:**
- **Synthetic** (unit tests): a disc beside an undetected bar, a disc on a smooth dark gradient, a bump on a
  ligament. Each must be sized within ±10% or rejected, never run away.
- **Beads:** the static and falling beads and the pours must be unchanged. Beads are isolated, so the tests must
  not fire on them.
- **The 20 hand-labelled frames:** matched droplets' sizes against the labels.
- **CCD sample (123534, 143415, 125754):** no sized droplet > 1.6× its mask.

### 2. Rolling background

  stored in `raw/background_rolling/` (~100 MB a run).

- **Provenance** records the background kind and block size.
- **The window mask** is built from every static dark piece, droplets included; droplets inside it are left out.
- **A window-drop log:** the frame at which a static object appears. A warning in the report, and later in GUI_Clean
  ("clean the window").

    neighbours';

### 3. Throughput (shared function)

**The liquid stage writes, per frame and inside the measurement volume:**
- the liquid volume V_liquid: droplets of every size from their masks, plus ligaments and blobs as bodies of
  revolution;
- the droplet volume;
- V_band: the 3.0.0+ sizes of sized droplets with lo ≤ d < hi, border-weighted.

**The quantities:**
- **In-band share** = Σ V_band / Σ V_liquid.
- **In-band liquid flow** (mL/min) = Q_syringe × share.
- **In-band droplets per second** = that flow / (π/6 ⟨d³⟩ of in-band droplets).
- No assumption about droplets below d_min.
- **95% CI** by bootstrap over frames.

**`rates.py` (numpy only), used by:**
- GUI_Clean (master_log);
- the app: responses `rate_band` (new formula) and `band_flow` (mL/min); band default 30-60;
- a throughput block in the run's liquid summary.

**Assumptions stated with every number:**
- every piece of liquid crosses the window at the same speed (snapshot = flux; the velocity to-do removes this);
- one window stands for the spray;
- ligament volumes come from 2-D;
- only droplets the AI finds (a missed droplet counts as liquid, lowering the share).

**Phase 3 as built (2026-10-17).**
- **`rates.py`** (numpy only): `syringe_mm3_s`, `band_parts`, `throughput` (95% CIs by resampling frames) and
  `from_run_files` (what GUI_Clean will call in Phase 4).
  - In-band share = in-band droplet volume ÷ all the liquid's volume inside the measurement volume.
  - In-band flow = Q × share (mL/min).
  - In-band droplets/s = Q × in-band droplets per mm³ of liquid in view.
  - Crowded and other unmeasured droplets are added back in proportion (the concentration's factor).
- **The liquid stage at 3.1.0** writes per frame `vol_sized / vol_below_d_min / vol_unmeasured / vol_unatomised /
  vol_droplets / vol_liquid _in_volume_mm3`, plus pooled values and `unmeasured_size_factor` in the summary. Each
  piece is counted by its best size:
  - sized droplets: calibrated size;
  - droplets below d_min: their measured size;
  - unmeasured droplets: mask size × the run's median size/mask ratio (0.63-0.68);
  - ligaments and blobs: bodies of revolution.
- **The app:**
  - `rate_band` and `rate` at 3.1.0 come from rates.py;
  - new `band_flow` (mL/min);
  - 3.0.0 runs get no rate (known wrong);
  - the band default is 30-60 µm (run_metrics, Settings).
- **GUI_Clean's master_log column** moves to rates.py in Phase 4, with the layout change.
- **CCD at 3.1.0** (scratch, the syringe speed from each run's name):

  | run | syringe | in-band share | in-band flow | droplets/s 30-60 µm (95% CI) | 2.2.0 column (20-60, old formula) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 123534 | 14.2 mL/min | 0.07% | 0.010 mL/min | 4,500 (2,300-12,000) | 26,300 |
  | 143415 | 14.2 mL/min | 0.29% | 0.041 mL/min | 19,400 (11,900-35,500) | 67,100 |
  | 125754 | 11.4 mL/min | 1.37% | 0.156 mL/min | 77,300 (51,600-127,000) | 223,400 |

  - **Consistency check on 125754:** ~22 in-band droplets in ~0.05 mm³ of liquid per frame × 190 mm³/s ≈ 80,000/s,
    which agrees.
  - **The CIs are wide (±50-60%).** The liquid volume in view is heavy-tailed (a thick ligament or jet in some
    frames carries most of it). More frames (the cines hold 10× the stride-10 set) would narrow them.
  - **By volume inside the volume, atomised is only 0.2-2.7%.** Ligaments and jets hold nearly all the liquid; by area
    it is 17-42%. Both numbers lean on the 2-D volume estimate (±40% absolute, earlier note).

## Velocity (separate; needs a capture)

- **The current cines cannot give velocity.**
  - 390 fps → 2.56 ms between frames.
  - At 4-8 m/s a droplet moves 10-20 mm, more than the 11.5 mm field, so none appears twice.
- **No hand-matching would be needed with a fast enough capture.** Tracking (PTV) matches droplets by predicted
  position, size and a consistent 3-4-frame path; a few dozen matches are checked by eye.
- **Route** (`docs/VELOCITY_CAPTURE_PLAN.md`):
  1. A 20-min exposure series: streak length gives speed with no new hardware.
  2. A narrow window at ~50 kfps for PTV.
- **It then gives:**
  - each size's speed (flux weighting of the size distribution and of droplets against ligaments);
  - a closure check: the measured liquid flux should add up to the syringe flow;
  - a cross-check of the mass-balance rate: in-band concentration × in-band speed × spray area.
