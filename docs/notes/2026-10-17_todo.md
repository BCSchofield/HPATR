## 3. Should droplets/s go, now that we have concentration? (Ben's question)

**Answer: keep droplets/s. They measure different things.**
- **Droplets/s (master_log column N)** is the production rate by mass balance:
  - syringe flow × atomised fraction by volume / (π/6 ⟨d³⟩), × the share in the band;
  - it is the throughput you're optimising (the collected, size-filtered spray: in-band droplets produced per
    second, Ben 2026-10-13);
  - it does not depend on where the camera looks or how deep the volume is.
- **Concentration** is a snapshot: how many droplets are in each cm3 at the moment of the frame.
  - It is about rate ÷ speed: faster gas spreads the same droplets thinner.
  - The cines cannot give droplet speed (`2026-10-13_droplet_rate_investigation.md`), so concentration cannot be
    turned into a rate.
  - Use it to compare sprays at similar gas speed, and as a check on the mass balance, not as the objective.

**But droplets/s is wrong at 3.0.0 as it stands. Fix it before relying on it.**
- **The band 20-60 um straddles d_min 30 um.** At 3.0.0 nothing below 30 um is sized, so the "20-60" column would
  in fact be "30-60".
- **The mass balance divides the atomised volume by ⟨d³⟩ of the SIZED droplets only.**
  - On 125754 the unsized droplets are 84% of the count. They hold an estimated 45-60% of the droplet volume
    (if they are 25-30 um).
  - Giving all that volume to the sized droplets roughly **doubles** the sized droplets' rate.
  - The same formula is in GUI_Clean (`droplet_rate_band`) and the app (`run_metrics.mass_balance_rate`).
- **The atomised fraction by volume should be the in-volume one,** for the same reason as by area. The liquid
  stage does not yet write an in-volume figure by volume.

**DECIDE:** the band at 3.0.0. Either 30-60 um (measurable now), or keep 20-60 by counting the below-d_min droplets
in it at an assumed size. 30-60 is recommended until the reticle's small dots extend d_min down.

## 6. Filaments and blobs that are partly in focus (Ben: "look at just the sections that DO fall into the focus band ... splitting them up partially?") — to think about

**Today** each filament or blob gets one blur width (`blur.object_chi`: area between the 0.77 and 0.25 levels /
perimeter, over the whole outline) and is wholly in or wholly out. A ligament tilted in depth, sharp at one end
and blurred at the other, is judged on its average.

**Idea.**
- Measure the blur width **locally along the outline**:
  - the same area-between-levels / perimeter, but in short segments of the outline (~20-30 px of edge each, enough
    to average the noise);
  - or per branch of the skeleton.
- Give each part of the object's area the verdict of its nearest outline segment. The in-volume liquid area then
  counts only the part inside.

**Things to settle first.**
- **The noise in χ from a short segment.**
  - Simulate tilted bars with a known χ gradient.
  - Check on the reticle: a large square tilted through focus gives an edge whose χ changes along it.
- **How short a segment can be** before χ is too noisy to judge.
- **Whether it matters.** First count how many filaments have a large χ spread along their outline. If few do,
  the whole-object verdict stands.
- **The numerator has to match.** A droplet is all in or all out, so only filaments and blobs would be split.

## 7. Does a change of camera frame size break anything? (Ben's question)

**Answer (code audit, 2026-10-16):** nothing crashes, and sizes and concentration stay right. What goes wrong is
comparing runs of different frame sizes, which nothing guards against. **Until the fixes below, keep one ROI
(same size and position) for every run analysed together.**

**Fine as it is.**
- **The AI** works on 800 px tiles placed from the real frame shape (6 tiles at 2048 × 1152, 12 at 2560 × 1600).
  Small frames are padded, never rescaled.
- **Sizing and the χ test** are per droplet.
- **The concentration's volume** uses the run's real frame area (`T.shape`), and the border weights use the real
  shape. The lens is telecentric, so the volume stays a box.
- **The window-drop mask** is built per run.
- **The droplets/s mass balance** is a ratio, so it does not depend on frame size.
- **The Focus check** searches the whole frame.
- **The window check** skips with a warning when the reference is a different size (it needs a new reference).

**Silently biased when runs differ in frame size.**
- **Counts per frame:** in-focus droplets per frame, the band count, and pieces and detections per frame all scale
  with frame area (`taguchi_analysis.per_frame`, `run_metrics.band_per_frame`, tables, drift plots).
- **Steadiness responses:** the count CV, intermittency and thin-frame share depend on how many droplets a frame
  holds. Lag-1 and the slow share depend on how long a droplet stays in view, which the ROI height sets.
- **No cross-run guard:** `stats.py`, `rsm_responses.py` and `compare_runs.py` check sizer, calibration and µm/px,
  never frame size.
- **The lamella crop in GUI_Clean** is fixed pixels (860, 829, 307, 583). A re-centred Phantom ROI measures the
  wrong place.
- **Physically:** D32 and the atomised fraction depend on which part of the spray is in view.

**Fixes.**
1. Record frame width and height:
   - in summary.json provenance;
   - in run_summary.xlsx Metadata, with the exposure;
   - in extraction_metadata.json.
   Add them to `compare_runs` keys, and make `stats` / `rsm_responses` refuse or warn when runs differ.
2. Count responses per mm² of frame (or per mm³), not per frame. Mark the steadiness responses as frame-size
   dependent.
3. The lamella crop: warn when the ROI changes or the crop falls outside the frame.
4. The background median is reused because the file exists: tie it to the frame shape.
5. **A bug found on the way:** measure_run's summary.json concentration divides by the frames that have
   detections (`len(rows)`), not all frames.
   - A sparse run (or a smaller ROI) with empty frames reads high.
   - The app's `conc` uses every frame and is right.
   - On the CCD day every frame of every run has detections, so it has no effect there (checked).
   - Fix: divide by `len(name_by_id)`.
6. Remove the dead fallbacks (`measure_run.concentration`'s 2048 × 1152, `run_metrics.FRAME_PX`).

## 7b. Weight each size by its speed (Ben, 2026-10-17)

- **Why.** The camera sees a snapshot. A collector gets a flux. Sizes that move faster are under-represented in the
  snapshot.
- **Weighting.** Weight each droplet by v(d) / v̄, and droplets against ligaments likewise, to turn the snapshot
  size distribution and the throughput into flux-weighted ones.
- **Needs** the velocity capture (`docs/VELOCITY_CAPTURE_PLAN.md`; the current 390 fps cines cannot give velocity:
  droplets move 10-20 mm between frames, more than the field).
- **Closure check:** the measured liquid flux should add up to the syringe flow.
